Q: How much does a storage heater cost to run overnight on Economy 7?

A: A typical 2 kW storage heater charging for 7 hours overnight uses 14 kWh. At Economy 7 off-peak rates of around 13p/kWh (check get_active_tariff() for current rates — rates here are illustrative only) this costs approximately £1.82 per night. On a flat tariff at 24p/kWh the same charge would cost £3.36.
