Q: What is an MPAN and MPRN?

A: MPAN (Meter Point Administration Number) is a unique 21-digit number identifying your electricity supply point — printed on your electricity bill. MPRN (Meter Point Reference Number) identifies your gas supply point. Both are used when switching suppliers.
