Q: What is thermal mass in the context of home heating?

A: Thermal mass refers to materials (concrete, brick, stone, water tanks) that absorb, store, and slowly release heat. High thermal mass slows temperature swings — helpful for heat pumps running at low flow temperatures and for Economy 7 storage heaters. Low thermal mass means rooms heat and cool quickly.
