Q: What is apparent power vs real power?

A: Real power (W) is the actual energy delivered to and consumed by appliances. Apparent power (VA or W in single-phase) is the product of RMS voltage and RMS current, including reactive power. For resistive loads (heaters, incandescent bulbs) they are equal. The IDEAL dataset records apparent power for whole-home electricity.
