Q: What is grid carbon intensity and why does it matter?

A: Grid carbon intensity (gCO2/kWh) measures how much CO2 is emitted per unit of electricity generated, depending on the mix of power sources at a given moment. Running appliances when intensity is low (high wind/solar generation) is better for the environment. The National Grid ESO publishes 30-minute intensity data at api.carbonintensity.org.uk.
