Q: What is a ground-source heat pump?

A: A ground-source heat pump (GSHP) extracts low-temperature heat from the ground via pipes buried horizontally or vertically. It is more efficient than an air-source heat pump (ASHP) in cold weather but requires more land or a deep borehole. BUS provides £7,500 towards both ASHP and GSHP.
