Q: What does the Ofgem price cap mean for my bill?

A: The Ofgem Default Tariff Cap limits the unit rate (p/kWh) and standing charge (p/day) suppliers can charge on variable tariffs. It does NOT cap the total bill — if you use more energy you pay more. The cap is reviewed and updated quarterly.
