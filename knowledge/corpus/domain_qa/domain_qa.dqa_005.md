Q: When is cheapest to charge an EV in the UK?

A: On Economy 7 or dedicated EV tariffs (e.g., Intelligent Octopus Go, OVO Charge Anytime), the cheapest window is typically 00:30–07:30. On Agile Octopus, half-hourly prices vary — negative price slots often occur overnight when wind generation is high. Check your tariff or use the Carbon Intensity API to identify low-carbon, low-cost slots.
