Q: What is a heat battery?

A: A heat battery (also called thermal storage unit) stores electrical energy as heat using a high-temperature storage medium. It can be charged at off-peak electricity rates and discharged to meet heating and hot water demand throughout the day. Added to the BUS grant scheme in late 2025.
