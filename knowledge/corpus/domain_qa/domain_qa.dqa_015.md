Q: What is the Coefficient of Performance (CoP) of a heat pump?

A: CoP is the ratio of heat output to electrical energy input. A CoP of 3 means the heat pump delivers 3 kWh of heat for every 1 kWh of electricity consumed. Typical air-source heat pumps achieve a seasonal CoP (SCOP) of 2.5–3.5 in UK climates. Ground-source heat pumps typically achieve 3.0–4.5.
