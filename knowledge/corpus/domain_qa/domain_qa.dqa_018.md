Q: What is an EPC and what are the bands?

A: An Energy Performance Certificate (EPC) rates a home's energy efficiency from A (most efficient) to G (least efficient). It is required when a property is sold, let, or newly built. Band A is the top 3% of homes; band G is the least efficient. Many UK homes are band D or E.
