Q: What is a Demand Response (DR) event?

A: A Demand Response event is a signal from a grid operator or energy supplier asking consumers to reduce or shift electricity consumption at times of high grid stress. In Octopus Energy these are called Saving Sessions. Participation is voluntary and usually rewarded with bill credits.
