Q: What is the Feed-in Tariff and is it still available?

A: The Feed-in Tariff (FiT) paid households for electricity generated by solar panels or other microgeneration. It closed to new applicants on 31 March 2019 and has been replaced by the Smart Export Guarantee (SEG). Existing FiT recipients continue to receive payments.
