Q: How does Economy 7 work?

A: Economy 7 is a time-of-use electricity tariff with two rates: a cheaper off-peak rate (typically 00:30–07:30 local time) and a higher peak rate during the remaining hours. It suits homes with storage heaters or EV charging overnight. The exact off-peak window varies by region and Distribution Network Operator (DNO).
