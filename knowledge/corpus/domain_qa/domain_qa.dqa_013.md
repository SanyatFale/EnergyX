Q: What is a smart meter In-Home Display (IHD)?

A: An IHD is a small wireless screen supplied with a smart meter that shows near-real-time energy consumption in kWh and £. It receives data from the meter via a Home Area Network (HAN). IHDs typically update every 10 seconds for electricity.
