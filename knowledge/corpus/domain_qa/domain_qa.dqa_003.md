Q: What is the difference between kW and kWh?

A: kW (kilowatts) measures power — the rate of energy use at a moment in time. kWh (kilowatt-hours) measures energy — the total amount used over a period. A 2 kW heater running for 3 hours uses 6 kWh of energy.
