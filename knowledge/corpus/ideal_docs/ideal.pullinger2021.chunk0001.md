Temperature readings in the IDEAL dataset are stored as integers in tenths of a degree Celsius. For example, a raw value of 205 represents 20.5°C. Room sensors sample at 12-second cadence. Boiler pipe temperatures are also recorded but their exact cadence should be verified against the readme.txt.
